Application process

How to apply for an external module

A clear, UCAS-style process — built so you can apply to any partner university module using a single reusable profile.

1

Build your profile

Create your reusable UCAS-style profile: personal details, academic history, personal statement and a referee. Save it once, reuse it forever.

2

Browse & search modules

Filter by subject, level, credits, location, delivery method and dates. Save modules you love to a shortlist for later.

3

Check pre-approval

Each module is labelled Pre-approved or Not pre-approved for credit transfer. For non-pre-approved, knowva initiates university-to-university approval on your behalf.

4

Apply with one profile

Hit apply. Your profile is sent securely to the host university. Track status updates — received, under review, decision — in your dashboard.

5

Receive decision & enrol

Universities respond with Accept, Conditional, Request info or Decline. Accept your offer and we'll guide enrolment at the host university.

01

Eligibility

  • Registered at a Joint Teaching Partnership university.
  • Eligible for the Lifelong Learning Entitlement (LLE).
  • Meet the module's entry requirements (shown on each listing).
  • Within your course switch window (this term: closes 18 Nov).
02

Documents

  • Photo ID (passport / driving licence).
  • Most recent academic transcript.
  • Personal statement (max 4,000 characters).
  • One academic referee contact.
  • Home university credit-transfer form (only if module is not pre-approved).
03

Deadlines

  • Switch window: open until 18 November.
  • Each module sets its own application deadline.
  • Decisions issued within 4–6 weeks.
  • Accept/decline offers within 14 days.
  • Late applications may be waitlisted.

What is the Joint Teaching Partnership?

A formal accreditation framework between UK universities that allows students to study individual modules at partner institutions, with credit automatically recognised at their home university.

How does the LLE cover this?

The Lifelong Learning Entitlement funds modules short of a full degree. knowva modules are fully LLE-eligible — your application links directly to your LLE account.

What is course switching?

During the switch window each term, students may add or swap an external module. Outside the window, only viewing and saving is enabled — applications reopen next term.

What if a module isn't pre-approved?

You can still apply. knowva routes a credit-transfer request between your home and host university automatically — you'll see live approval status on your application.
